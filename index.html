How Indian is “Made in India”, really?

Ten trackers that take apart a product India wants to build for itself, score every layer for Indian ownership of design, manufacturing and supply, and name the companies, the money, the gaps and the next steps. Each answers the same question: how far is India from a version that is Indian all the way down?

How the trackers score

Each tracker scores the product's layers from 0 to 100 for Indian ownership of design IP, manufacturing capability and supply control, then weights them by an indicative bill of materials for a typical product. The headline is the Indian-owned share of that value. Scores are editorial estimates from public sources as of 28 October 2026, meant to compare layers and products, not official statistics. Each tracker lists its sources and flags figures it could not confirm.
